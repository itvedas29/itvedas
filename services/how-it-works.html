How It Works

A straightforward path from "we have a problem" to "it's handled" — with a clear stop before anything is paid for.

01

Tell us the problem

Fill out the requirement form. Describe what's happening in plain English — you don't need to know the technical cause. Tell us your environment (roughly how many users and devices, what platforms you're on) and your budget range.

02

We review it

We read every requirement and assess what's actually going on. If we need more detail, we'll reach out using the contact method you provided. We won't promise a specific response time here — urgent requests are prioritized, but this isn't a 24/7 service.

03

Get a practical solution

We come back with a scoped recommendation: what needs to happen, roughly how long it will take, and a price that fits (or explains why) your stated budget. Nothing is billed at this stage.

04

Start the project

For this initial phase, once you agree to proceed, the project is set up and paid for through Fiverr or Upwork — not paid directly to ITVedas Services. This keeps your payment covered by their buyer protection terms while we deliver the work.

Why Fiverr or Upwork for payment?

ITVedas Services doesn't process payments directly in this initial phase. Routing projects through an established freelance platform means your payment is protected by their terms, and you get a familiar dispute-resolution process if anything goes wrong. As the platform matures, direct invoicing may be introduced — this page will be updated if that changes.

Ready to get started?

Tell us what's going on — there's no cost or commitment to submit a requirement.
