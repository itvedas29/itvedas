About

Independent IT Support, Built on Real Experience

ITVedas Services is an independent, remote-first IT support practice — part of the wider ITVedas knowledge platform.

Who's behind this

ITVedas Services is currently operated as an independent IT specialist practice, not a large team or agency. Requirements are reviewed personally, and work is delivered with direct, hands-on involvement rather than handed off through account managers.

The technical background behind ITVedas Services spans day-to-day IT operations and hands-on work across:

Endpoint Management Cybersecurity Active Directory Microsoft 365 Google Workspace Windows macOS VMware Patch Management JumpCloud ManageEngine Endpoint Central CrowdStrike Jamf

How we work

  • ✓Every requirement is reviewed by a person, not routed through a queue.
  • ✓We say plainly when something is outside our expertise, rather than taking it on regardless.
  • ✓Scope and price are agreed before work starts — no surprise invoices.
  • ✓Remote-first by design, not as a limitation.

Part of ITVedas

ITVedas Services sits alongside ITVedas, a knowledge platform covering networking, cloud, security, DevOps and IT operations, plus a set of free diagnostic tools. This site is the hands-on services side of that same effort.
