How Payment Works

1

We agree on scope and price

After reviewing your requirement, we confirm exactly what's included and the price.

2

Payment happens through Fiverr or Upwork

For this initial phase, projects are started and paid for through Fiverr or Upwork — not directly to ITVedas Services. This keeps payments protected by their buyer terms.
