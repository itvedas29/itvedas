IT Migration Services

Moving email providers, endpoint management platforms, or core infrastructure is risky to get wrong. We plan and run migrations so nothing gets lost in transit.

Request IT Migration Help View Pricing Guidance

Who is this for?

  • ✓Businesses switching email or productivity platforms (e.g. Google Workspace to Microsoft 365, or the reverse)
  • ✓Companies changing endpoint management or MDM providers
  • ✓Teams outgrowing an ad-hoc setup and consolidating onto proper tooling
  • ✓Anyone who needs a migration planned before committing to a cutover date

Common problems

  • !Uncertainty about what will break during a platform switch
  • !Email or files at risk of being lost mid-migration

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you migrate us without downtime?

Most migrations can be planned to minimize disruption, but we won't promise zero downtime until we've seen your specific environment — we'll tell you honestly what to expect.

What platforms have you migrated between?

Common cases include Google Workspace <-> Microsoft 365, and switching between endpoint management / MDM providers such as ManageEngine, JumpCloud and Jamf.

Do you handle the DNS changes involved?

Yes, DNS and domain cutover planning is part of a typical migration engagement.

What happens if something breaks after cutover?

We plan a rollback path before starting, and this service includes post-migration verification specifically to catch issues early.

Ready to fix your IT Migration issue?

Tell us what's going on — we'll review it and get back to you with next steps.
