FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do we need special software for this?

Not necessarily — for smaller fleets a well-structured tracking system may be enough. We'll recommend tooling only if your scale justifies it.

Can this tie into our endpoint management platform?

Yes, if you already have one (e.g. Endpoint Central or JumpCloud), asset inventory can often be pulled from it directly.

Can you help us figure out which licenses we're not using?

Yes, this is one of the most common outcomes of an asset audit — identifying unused or duplicate licenses to cut cost.

Does this include physical asset recovery when someone leaves?

We can help design the offboarding process, but physical shipping/collection logistics are handled by you unless otherwise agreed.
