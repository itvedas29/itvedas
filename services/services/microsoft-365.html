Microsoft 365 Support for Small Businesses

From mailboxes that stopped receiving mail to licensing you're not sure you still need, we help small teams run Microsoft 365 without a dedicated admin.

Request Microsoft 365 Help View Pricing Guidance

Who is this for?

  • ✓Businesses running Microsoft 365 without an in-house Microsoft 365 admin
  • ✓Teams whose email, Teams or SharePoint stopped working as expected
  • ✓Companies onboarding or offboarding employees and need accounts handled correctly
  • ✓Anyone unsure what they're actually paying for across their license tiers

Common problems

  • !Users not receiving email, or mail going to the wrong place
  • !MFA locking people out, or not being enforced at all
  • !Outlook stuck syncing, duplicating, or refusing to send
  • !Former employees still holding active licenses and mailbox access

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to give you full admin access to my tenant?

No more than is needed for the specific task. We'll ask for the minimum access required and talk through it with you before starting.

Can you help if we only have a few Microsoft 365 licenses?

Yes — most of our Microsoft 365 requests come from teams with under 30 users and no dedicated IT admin.

What if I don't know what's actually wrong?

That's normal. Describe the symptom in the requirement form and we'll diagnose the cause during review.

Do you work with Exchange hybrid or on-prem setups?

We primarily support cloud-only Microsoft 365 tenants. Tell us your setup in the requirement form and we'll confirm fit before quoting.

Ready to fix your Microsoft 365 issue?

Tell us what's going on — we'll review it and get back to you with next steps.
