FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is support available 24/7?

No — we don't offer 24/7 coverage. Tell us your urgency level in the requirement form and we'll respond as quickly as we reasonably can.

Do you support employees outside the countries you list?

We're remote-first and work with international clients broadly. Mention your location in the requirement form and we'll confirm fit.

Can one employee use this without going through a company account?

For now, requirements come in per business, not per individual employee — a company contact submits the request.

What if the issue turns out to need a different service (like Microsoft 365 or security)?

That's fine — remote support is often the entry point, and we'll route the requirement to the right scope during review.

Ready to fix your Remote IT Support issue?

Tell us what's going on — we'll review it and get back to you with next steps.
