Common problems

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a substitute for a dedicated security team?

No — this is practical hardening for teams without one. For regulated industries with formal compliance requirements, you may need a specialized firm in addition.

Can you help if we think we've already been compromised?

We can help assess and contain a suspected compromise, but for active large-scale incidents we'll be upfront if you need a specialized incident-response firm.

Do you offer a security certification or audit report?

No — we don't issue compliance certifications. We provide a practical assessment and remediation plan, not a formal audit.

What's the fastest thing we can do to reduce risk?

In most small businesses, enforcing MFA everywhere and fixing email authentication (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) closes the two biggest gaps first.
