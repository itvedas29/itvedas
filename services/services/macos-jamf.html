FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you support both Jamf Pro and Jamf Now?

Yes, we have experience with both, and can help you decide which fits your team size.

We only have a few Macs — is that too small?

No — Jamf Now in particular is built for small fleets, and we can also just support individual Macs without any MDM at all.

Can you help migrate from another MDM to Jamf?

Yes, this is treated as an IT migration project — describe your current setup in the requirement form.

Do you handle Apple Business Manager setup?

Yes, ABM/enrollment configuration is part of a typical Jamf setup engagement.
