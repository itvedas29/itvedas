New Awards Program Shines Spotlight on Industrial Cybersecurity Leaders

SecurityWeek introduces recognition program for OT security innovators, with winners announced at 2026 Nashville conference.

A New Recognition Program for Factory and Infrastructure Security

The industrial world just got a new way to celebrate its cybersecurity heroes. SecurityWeek, a respected publication covering digital threats, has launched a fresh awards program specifically designed to honor individuals, teams, and tools making a real difference in protecting factories, power grids, and other critical infrastructure from cyberattacks.

Think of industrial cybersecurity like the security guards at a bank, except instead of protecting money in vaults, these professionals guard the computers and networks that run everything from water treatment plants to manufacturing facilities. These systems are increasingly under attack, and the new awards aim to spotlight who's doing it right.

The program operates independently, meaning no single company paid to influence who wins. It's similar to how Consumer Reports rates products fairly without brand favoritism. Winners will be announced at the 2026 ICS Cybersecurity Conference happening in Nashville, giving the security community a major event to celebrate.

What This Means

This awards program signals something important: industrial cybersecurity has reached a level where excellence deserves formal recognition. For years, the spotlight focused mostly on protecting computers and networks in regular businesses. Industrial systems—the infrastructure society depends on—often got less attention.

The creation of this specific awards category suggests the security industry is taking operational technology (OT) threats more seriously than ever. These aren't academic achievements; the program specifically recognizes "proven impact," meaning winners must show actual results protecting real systems.

  • It validates the field: Workers in industrial cybersecurity now have a prestigious career milestone to pursue
  • It encourages innovation: Technology companies will likely invest more in solutions targeting this sector
  • It creates standards: The criteria for winning helps define what "excellence" looks like in this specialized area

Why You Should Care

If you work in manufacturing, utilities, chemical processing, or any industrial setting, this matters directly. When cybersecurity professionals and tools are recognized and celebrated, more talent flows into the field, and solutions improve faster.

If you're a consumer, this still affects you. A successful cyberattack on a power plant or water treatment facility could disrupt your daily life. Recognition programs help attract and retain the specialized experts needed to prevent those scenarios.

The security of industrial systems touches nearly every aspect of modern life, from the electricity powering your home to the water you drink.

Organizations in the industrial sector should also pay attention. These awards highlight which companies and individuals are leading the charge, making it easier to identify who can help secure your own systems.

What You Can Do

If you work in industrial cybersecurity or aspire to enter the field, learn about the award criteria. They typically signal what the industry values most, helping guide your professional development.

If you lead an organization running critical infrastructure, consider nominating your team or the vendors you work with. Participation creates visibility and helps establish your organization as serious about protection.

Stay tuned for more details about the 2026 awards program—the criteria, nomination process, and winner announcements will all offer valuable insights into what separates average security work from genuinely transformative efforts.

Industrial cybersecurity excellence finally has a stage where it belongs.
