ManageEngine Identity360: Cloud-Native Workforce IAM Compared

AD360 is built for hybrid estates still anchored to on-prem Active Directory. Identity360 is ManageEngine's cloud-native answer for organizations that don't want an on-prem identity dependency at all. Here's how it compares to the established cloud IdPs.

Key Facts
  • Identity360 is cloud-only, with a built-in Universal Directory rather than syncing from on-prem AD — a fundamentally different architecture from AD360
  • The cloud IdP market (Okta, Entra ID, JumpCloud) is mature and competitive, so a newer entrant needs a genuinely clear reason to switch, not just feature parity
  • SSO, MFA, and role-based access control are table stakes across this whole category — what differs is depth of app integrations and orchestration flexibility

What It Does

ManageEngine Identity360 provides identity lifecycle management, orchestration with smart templates, MFA for apps and endpoints, SSO for enterprise applications, role-based access control, and built-in reporting — all cloud-native, with its own Universal Directory rather than syncing from on-prem AD.

Who It's For

Cloud-first organizations evaluating a workforce identity platform without on-prem AD dependency, particularly if already using other ManageEngine or Zoho products and want identity in the same ecosystem. If your organization still has meaningful on-prem AD investment, AD360 is the more natural fit.

How It Compares

ManageEngine Identity360

Cloud-native IAM with built-in directory, SSO, MFA, and orchestration — a newer entrant in a mature category, priced to be accessible for organizations not wanting the largest incumbent IdP's cost or complexity.

Okta

The category leader — the deepest app-integration catalog (thousands of pre-built connectors) and the most mature orchestration/workflow engine of the group. Also priced and positioned as the premium option; per-user costs add up at scale.

Microsoft Entra ID

The default if you're already on Microsoft 365 — identity is effectively bundled into the ecosystem you're already paying for. The natural first evaluation for any Microsoft-centric organization before looking elsewhere.

JumpCloud

A cloud directory platform with a strong reputation among smaller/mid-size IT teams for combining directory, SSO, and device management at a more accessible price point than Okta. A reasonable direct comparison point for Identity360 in the mid-market cloud-IAM segment.

Bottom Line

  • Already on Microsoft 365? Entra ID is the path of least resistance before evaluating anything else.
  • Need the deepest app-integration catalog and most mature workflow engine, budget isn't the constraint? Okta.
  • Mid-market team wanting cloud directory + SSO + device management at a more accessible price? JumpCloud and Identity360 are the closer comparison.
  • Already invested in the ManageEngine/Zoho ecosystem and want identity in the same family of tools? Identity360 is the natural fit to evaluate first.
