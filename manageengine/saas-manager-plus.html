    ManageEngine SaaS Manager Plus: SaaS Management Compared

    Every department signing up for its own SaaS tools with a company credit card is how "shadow IT" and duplicate subscriptions quietly become a real budget problem. SaaS management platforms exist to surface that spend. Here's how the options compare.

    Key Facts
    • SaaS Manager Plus discovers SaaS applications in use across the organization — including shadow IT that never went through procurement
    • Unused/underutilized subscription identification is often the direct, quantifiable ROI that justifies the tool
    • Automatic invoice retrieval from mail reduces the manual finance work of tracking scattered SaaS billing

    What It Does

    ManageEngine SaaS Manager Plus provides SaaS application discovery, user activity and app utilization tracking, vendor spending and renewal date monitoring, identification of unused or underutilized subscriptions, out-of-the-box integration with a range of third-party applications, and automatic invoice retrieval from mail.

    Who It's For

    IT and finance teams that want visibility into SaaS spend and usage across the organization — particularly useful for cutting waste from unused licenses and catching shadow IT that never went through formal procurement.

    How It Compares

    ManageEngine SaaS Manager Plus

    SaaS discovery, usage tracking, and spend/renewal monitoring with automatic invoice retrieval, priced accessibly for mid-size organizations wanting SaaS visibility without a dedicated enterprise SaaS management platform.

    Zylo

    A category leader in enterprise SaaS management, with deep discovery and optimization capability aimed at large organizations with significant SaaS sprawl. Enterprise pricing and positioning to match its depth.

    Torii

    Strong automated discovery and workflow automation for SaaS onboarding/offboarding, popular with mid-market IT teams. A close direct comparison to SaaS Manager Plus in scope and target market.

    BetterCloud

    Broader SaaS operations platform that extends into automated user lifecycle management and security policy enforcement across SaaS apps, not just discovery/spend tracking. A bigger platform commitment than pure SaaS spend visibility.

    Bottom Line

    • Want SaaS discovery and spend visibility at accessible pricing? SaaS Manager Plus or Torii — worth comparing directly.
    • Large enterprise with significant SaaS sprawl needing deep optimization? Zylo.
    • Want SaaS management plus automated user lifecycle and security policy enforcement? BetterCloud goes further.
