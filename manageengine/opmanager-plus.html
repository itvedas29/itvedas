ManageEngine OpManager Plus: Full-Stack Observability Compared

OpManager watches your network. OpManager Plus tries to watch everything — network, servers, applications, and the end-user experience, with AIOps stitching the alerts together. Here's how that converged pitch compares to the alternatives.

Key Facts
  • OpManager Plus bundles OpManager (network monitoring) with application performance, IT infrastructure, and data center monitoring into one converged suite
  • "AIOps" here means automated correlation of related alerts into a single incident, not autonomous remediation — reduces alert noise rather than replacing an admin
  • It's ManageEngine's answer to the "unified observability platform" category that SolarWinds, Datadog, and others also compete in

What It Does

ManageEngine OpManager Plus converges IT operations management, IT infrastructure management, unified network management, and data center infrastructure management into one suite, with AIOps-driven alert correlation and digital experience monitoring layered on top of OpManager's core network monitoring.

ManageEngine OpManager Plus

Who It's For

Teams that have outgrown OpManager's network-only scope and want converged network + server + application visibility from one vendor without adopting a separate cloud-native observability platform. If you only need network monitoring specifically, plain OpManager is the leaner, cheaper starting point.

How It Compares

ManageEngine OpManager Plus

Converged network, server, and application observability with AIOps alert correlation, on-prem deployment as a genuine option, and pricing aimed at mid-market IT operations teams rather than the largest cloud-native shops.

SolarWinds Hybrid Cloud Observability

SolarWinds' own converged observability play, built on the mature Orion platform. Comparable positioning and similarly aimed at enterprises already invested in on-prem infrastructure monitoring; pricing scales with the breadth of modules enabled.

Datadog Infrastructure Monitoring

The cloud-native default for engineering-led organizations, with the broadest integration ecosystem of the group. Consumption-based pricing and a design center of gravity around cloud/container workloads rather than traditional on-prem network gear.

Auvik

Cloud-based network monitoring built with MSPs in mind — fast deployment, strong auto-discovery. Narrower in scope than OpManager Plus (network-focused, not full-stack observability), but worth a look if speed of MSP-style rollout matters more than converged breadth.

Bottom Line

  • Want converged network + server + app monitoring from one vendor, with on-prem as an option? OpManager Plus.
  • Already deep in the SolarWinds Orion ecosystem? Its Hybrid Cloud Observability keeps you in-platform.
  • Cloud/container-native engineering org? Datadog fits that workload profile better.
  • MSP wanting fast network-focused rollout across many client networks? Auvik is the more specialized, lighter-weight option.
