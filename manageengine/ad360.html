ManageEngine AD360: Identity Governance Across On-Prem AD and Entra ID

Hybrid identity estates mean juggling the on-prem AD console, the Entra admin center, and Microsoft Graph scripts just to answer "who has access to what, and why." AD360 is one of a handful of platforms built to unify that view. Here's what it does and how it compares.

Key Facts
  • AD360 combines user provisioning, self-service password reset, MFA, and UEBA-driven auditing across on-prem AD, Entra ID, and Microsoft 365 in one console
  • It's an identity governance platform, not a directory itself — it sits on top of your existing AD/Entra ID and manages them, rather than replacing either
  • Best fit for mid-to-large organizations running genuinely hybrid identity (both on-prem AD and Entra ID matter), not pure cloud-native shops

What AD360 Actually Does

AD360 bundles several ManageEngine identity products — ADManager Plus (provisioning), ADSelfService Plus (self-service/MFA), ADAudit Plus (auditing), Exchange Reporter Plus, and M365 Manager Plus — into one integrated console, plus its own identity analytics and access certification layer on top. The pitch is consolidation: instead of running five separate point tools against your directory, you get one pane of glass for identity lifecycle management, self-service, and compliance reporting spanning both on-prem AD and the Microsoft cloud stack.

That consolidation matters most for teams that have already outgrown "just use PowerShell" (see our PowerShell guides if you're not there yet) but don't want to stitch together five separate ManageEngine licenses and logins to get provisioning, self-service, and auditing working together.

ManageEngine AD360

Who It's For — and Who It Isn't

AD360 is built for IT/security teams managing a genuinely hybrid identity estate: on-prem Active Directory that's still authoritative for a meaningful chunk of the org, synced into Entra ID via Entra Connect. If your organization is already cloud-only with no on-prem AD dependency, a lot of AD360's on-prem-facing modules (auditing file servers, on-prem provisioning workflows) simply won't apply, and Microsoft's own native Entra ID Governance is the more natural fit.

How It Compares

ManageEngine AD360

Unified console across on-prem AD, Entra ID, and Microsoft 365 — provisioning, self-service, MFA, and UEBA-driven auditing bundled together. Strongest where an organization needs both on-prem AD depth and cloud identity governance in one tool, without paying for and learning five separate products.

Microsoft Entra ID Governance

Microsoft's own native add-on — access reviews, entitlement management, and lifecycle workflows built directly into Entra ID. The obvious first choice if your org is cloud-first and doesn't need deep on-prem AD auditing/provisioning in the same tool. Licensing is per-user via Entra ID P2/Governance add-on, which can be more cost-effective if you're already paying for P2.

Okta Identity Governance

Strong if Okta is already your identity provider — governance and access certification layered on top of an Okta-centric SSO/MFA deployment. Less natural fit if your organization's source of truth is still on-prem AD rather than Okta Universal Directory.

One Identity Manager (Quest)

The deepest, most customizable identity governance platform of the group — built for large enterprises with complex approval workflows and many downstream systems to provision into, not just AD/Entra ID/M365. Correspondingly the heaviest to implement and the most expensive; overkill for mid-size IT teams whose governance needs are mostly AD- and Microsoft-365-centric.

Bottom Line

  • Genuinely hybrid identity, want on-prem AD and Entra ID/M365 governance in one console? AD360 is built for exactly that.
  • Cloud-first, already paying for Entra ID P2? Start with Microsoft's native Entra ID Governance before adding a third-party tool.
  • Okta is your primary IdP? Okta Identity Governance keeps everything on one platform.
  • Large enterprise with complex, multi-system provisioning needs beyond AD/M365? One Identity Manager has the deepest customization, at a matching cost and complexity.
