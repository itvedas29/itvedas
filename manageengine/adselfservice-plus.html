ManageEngine ADSelfService Plus: Self-Service Password Reset and MFA

"I forgot my password" is one of the single biggest sources of helpdesk ticket volume at most organizations. Self-service password reset tools exist to take that off the queue entirely — here's how the leading options compare.

Key Facts
  • Password-reset tickets routinely account for a large share of total helpdesk ticket volume at mid-size and larger organizations
  • ADSelfService Plus covers self-service reset/unlock, password expiration reminders, and MFA for Windows login, VPN, OWA, and cloud apps
  • Self-service password reset (SSPR) and single sign-on/MFA are related but separate capabilities — some tools here specialize in one over the other

Why "Just Call the Helpdesk" Doesn't Scale

A locked-out or forgotten password is one of the most common — and least interesting — reasons someone contacts IT. It's also one of the most expensive to leave unaddressed at scale: every reset ticket costs helpdesk time, and users are locked out of work until someone picks it up. Self-service password reset tools let users verify their identity through pre-registered security questions, email/SMS/authenticator-app verification, or biometrics, then reset their own password or unlock their own account — no ticket, no wait.

ManageEngine ADSelfService Plus extends that same self-service model to also cover MFA for Windows logins, VPN, OWA/Outlook Web Access, and a range of cloud and on-prem applications, plus proactive password-expiration notifications so users change their password before it expires rather than getting locked out.

ManageEngine ADSelfService Plus

Who It's For

IT teams at organizations with a meaningful volume of AD-based password reset/unlock tickets who want to cut that volume and add broad MFA coverage without buying a separate, more expensive identity platform just for MFA. It's a strong fit specifically for orgs that are still AD-centric (not fully cloud-native), since ADSelfService Plus is built to sit directly on top of on-prem Active Directory.

How It Compares

ManageEngine ADSelfService Plus

Self-service reset/unlock, password-expiration reminders, and MFA spanning Windows login, VPN, OWA, and a broad range of on-prem and cloud apps — all built directly against on-prem Active Directory. Priced and licensed to be accessible for mid-size IT teams that need AD-centric self-service without a full identity platform purchase.

Microsoft Entra Self-Service Password Reset (SSPR)

Built into Entra ID and the natural default if your organization is already licensed for Entra ID P1/P2 — no separate tool to deploy. The tradeoff: it's scoped to Entra ID/hybrid-synced accounts and doesn't extend MFA coverage to the same breadth of on-prem, non-Microsoft applications that ADSelfService Plus covers out of the box.

Specops Password Reset (Outpost24)

A close direct competitor purpose-built for on-prem AD self-service reset, with particularly strong password-policy enforcement features (breached-password blocking, custom complexity rules) alongside the reset/unlock workflow. Worth comparing closely against ADSelfService Plus if AD password policy enforcement specifically is a priority alongside self-service.

Okta (Workflows + MFA)

Strong if you're already running Okta as your primary identity provider — self-service and MFA come as part of a broader SSO/identity platform rather than a standalone AD-focused tool. Less of a fit if your organization isn't otherwise moving toward Okta as its IdP, since it's a much bigger platform commitment than a dedicated SSPR tool.

Bottom Line

  • AD-centric org wanting self-service reset plus broad MFA coverage in one tool? ADSelfService Plus is built for exactly that.
  • Already paying for Entra ID P1/P2? Try native Entra SSPR first before adding a third-party tool.
  • Password policy enforcement (breached-password blocking, custom complexity) matters as much as reset itself? Compare closely against Specops Password Reset.
  • Already standardized on Okta as your identity provider? Its native self-service/MFA keeps everything on one platform.
