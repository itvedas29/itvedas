ManageEngine Patch Manager Plus: Patch Management Compared

If patching specifically — not broader device management — is your actual pain point, a dedicated patch tool is often a lighter, more focused pickup than a full UEM suite. Here's how the options compare.

Key Facts
  • Patch Manager Plus is the standalone patching module from the same family as Endpoint Central — for teams that want patching specifically, not full UEM
  • Third-party application patching (not just OS updates) is where most patch-management gaps actually show up
  • Patch compliance reporting matters as much as the patching itself for audit and security-posture purposes

What It Does

ManageEngine Patch Manager Plus provides automated patch management, customized deployment policies, third-party application patching, cross-platform support (Windows, macOS, Linux), patch compliance checks, and dynamic monitoring and reporting.

Who It's For

IT teams that specifically need reliable, automated patching — including third-party apps — without adopting a full UEM platform's broader device-management scope. If you need patching plus deployment, imaging, and remote control together, Endpoint Central bundles all of that in one product.

How It Compares

ManageEngine Patch Manager Plus

Focused, cross-platform patch management including third-party apps, with compliance reporting — priced as a point solution for teams that specifically need patching depth without a full UEM commitment.

Microsoft Intune

The default if you're already on Microsoft 365 — Windows Update management is native and free. The gap is third-party application patching, where Intune's native coverage is thinner than dedicated patch tools.

Automox

Cloud-native and patching-first, with a strong reputation specifically for fast, reliable OS and third-party patching across Windows, macOS, and Linux. A close direct comparison to Patch Manager Plus in the same "patching specialist" category.

NinjaOne

Broader RMM platform with patch management as one module among many (also does remote monitoring, scripting, ticketing). A bigger platform commitment than a point patch tool, popular particularly with MSPs managing multiple client environments.

Bottom Line

  • Already on Microsoft 365, Windows-only patching is enough? Intune's native patching covers it.
  • Need cross-platform patching including robust third-party app coverage, without a full UEM suite? Patch Manager Plus or Automox — worth comparing directly.
  • MSP or team wanting patching bundled with broader RMM capability? NinjaOne is the bigger platform.
  • Need patching plus deployment, imaging, and remote control together? Endpoint Central bundles all of it.
