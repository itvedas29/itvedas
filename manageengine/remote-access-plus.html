    ManageEngine Remote Access Plus: Enterprise Remote Support Compared

    Consumer remote-desktop tools work fine for one-off favors. IT teams supporting hundreds of endpoints need something built for scale, audit trails, and unattended access. Here's how the enterprise options compare.

    Key Facts
    • Enterprise remote support tools differ from consumer screen-sharing apps mainly in unattended access, audit logging, and integration with existing endpoint/asset inventories
    • Remote Access Plus is part of the same product family as Endpoint Central, sold standalone for teams that just need the remote-support piece
    • Voice/video/text chat built into the session is a real time-saver versus tabbing out to a separate call

    What It Does

    ManageEngine Remote Access Plus gives helpdesk and IT support staff remote control of end-user machines with diagnostic tools, file transfer, voice/video/text chat, and wake-on-LAN/remote shutdown, built for enterprise-scale unattended access rather than one-off ad hoc sessions.

    ManageEngine Remote Access Plus

    Who It's For

    IT helpdesk teams that need reliable, audited unattended remote access across a managed fleet — not occasional screen-sharing with an external client. If you need consumer-facing screen-share (supporting customers, not employees), a tool like TeamViewer QuickSupport is a better fit than an enterprise endpoint tool.

    How It Compares

    ManageEngine Remote Access Plus

    Built for internal IT support at scale — unattended access to managed endpoints, diagnostic tools, and integrated chat, priced for enterprise helpdesk use rather than per-technician consumer licensing.

    TeamViewer

    The best-known name in remote support, with broad platform support and both attended and unattended access. Licensing can get expensive at scale, and its consumer-detection heuristics have historically caused friction for legitimate business use.

    AnyDesk

    Lightweight, fast connection times, and generally more affordable than TeamViewer at scale. A reasonable alternative for teams that want simplicity over deep enterprise integration.

    BeyondTrust Remote Support

    The enterprise-security-focused option — session recording, granular permissions, and compliance features aimed at regulated industries. Heavier and pricier than the others here; makes sense when audit/compliance requirements are the primary driver.

    Bottom Line

    • Need reliable unattended access across a managed internal fleet? Remote Access Plus is built for exactly that.
    • Need broad cross-platform support and don't mind consumer-tool pricing at scale? TeamViewer or AnyDesk.
    • Compliance/audit requirements are the primary driver? BeyondTrust Remote Support has the deepest session-recording and permissions model.
