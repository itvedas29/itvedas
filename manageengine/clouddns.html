ManageEngine CloudDNS: Managed DNS Compared

Your own domain's DNS is one of the few single points of failure that can take your entire online presence down if it's slow, unreliable, or under attack. Here's how ManageEngine's managed DNS service compares to the established players.

Key Facts
  • Authoritative DNS hosting is a mature, commoditized category — the real differentiators are network size/latency, DDoS resilience, and price
  • CloudDNS offers automatic failover routing queries to the closest point of presence and DNSSEC support
  • This is a different product from DDI Central — CloudDNS is hosted/managed authoritative DNS; DDI Central is on-prem unified DNS/DHCP/IPAM management

What It Does

ManageEngine CloudDNS provides points of presence at global vantage points, DNS failover protection with automatic routing to the closest PoP, a robust redundant DNS network, fast query resolution (averaging around 30ms globally), DNSSEC support, and integrated monitoring with secondary DNS and failover strategies.

Who It's For

Businesses that want managed authoritative DNS hosting with global redundancy and failover, without running their own DNS infrastructure. If you specifically need on-prem DNS/DHCP/IPAM management rather than hosted authoritative DNS, DDI Central is the more relevant ManageEngine product.

How It Compares

ManageEngine CloudDNS

Managed authoritative DNS with global PoPs, automatic failover, and DNSSEC — priced to be accessible for businesses that want reliable managed DNS without enterprise-scale pricing.

Cloudflare DNS

One of the largest, fastest anycast DNS networks in the world, and free for basic use as part of Cloudflare's broader platform (CDN, security). The default reflex for most businesses today given the free tier and network scale.

AWS Route 53

The natural choice if your infrastructure already lives on AWS — tight integration with other AWS services (health checks, load balancers, S3). Pay-per-use pricing that's usually modest for standard DNS volumes.

NS1 (now part of IBM)

An enterprise-focused DNS platform known for advanced traffic-steering and application-aware DNS logic (useful for sophisticated multi-region/multi-cloud routing). Enterprise pricing and complexity aimed at larger organizations with advanced traffic-management needs.

Bottom Line

  • Want free, massive-scale anycast DNS with minimal setup? Cloudflare DNS is the default most businesses reach for first.
  • Already on AWS? Route 53 integrates most tightly with the rest of your infrastructure.
  • Need advanced traffic-steering/application-aware DNS logic at enterprise scale? NS1.
  • Want a straightforward managed DNS option outside the AWS/Cloudflare ecosystems? CloudDNS is worth a look.
