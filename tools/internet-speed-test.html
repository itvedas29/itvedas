    Internet Speed Test

    Check your connection's download speed, upload speed, and latency. For the most useful result, pause downloads, streaming, and other heavy network activity while the test runs.

    Ready to test your connection?

    A test transfers about 22 MB of data to Cloudflare's speed-test network.

    Your results will appear here.

    Latency -- ms
    Download -- Mbps
    Upload -- Mbps

    How the test works

    The test checks response time, then downloads and uploads small test files to Cloudflare's nearby speed-test network. Speeds are estimates for your connection to that network and can vary with Wi-Fi signal, device activity, and network congestion.

    The test runs in your browser and this page does not save your results. Test traffic goes to Cloudflare, which can receive your IP address and request metadata as part of serving the test.
