Quick Answer

Your public IP address is the address your router or device presents to the wider internet. This page shows it instantly, along with the country, region, and internet provider (ASN) Cloudflare's network sees it connecting from — no signup or third-party service required.

What is a public IP address?

A public IP address identifies your connection on the internet, similar to a mailing address. It's assigned by your Internet Service Provider (ISP) and is different from the private IP address (like 192.168.1.5) used inside your home or office network. Websites and services see your public IP, not your private one.

How this tool works

This page makes a request to ITVEDAS's own edge servers, which read the connection details Cloudflare's network already has for your request (IP address, coarse geolocation, ASN) and return them to your browser. No external IP-lookup service is called and no data is stored.

Privacy

Your IP and network details are read from the request itself and returned directly to you. Nothing is logged, stored, or shared with third parties.

FAQ

Why does my IP change?

Most home internet connections use dynamic IPs that your ISP can reassign periodically, or when you reconnect. Mobile connections and VPNs also change your visible IP frequently.

Does this show my IPv6 address too?

It shows whichever address (IPv4 or IPv6) your device used to reach ITVEDAS. If your network supports IPv6 and prefers it, you may see an IPv6 address here.

Is my public IP the same as my location?

Not precisely — geolocation from an IP is approximate and based on ISP registration data, typically accurate to city or region level, not an exact address.
