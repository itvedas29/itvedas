Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I use this checklist?
Use frequency depends on your environment. Critical items should be checked frequently (daily or weekly), while others can be checked monthly or quarterly.
Can I modify this checklist?
Yes! This is a template. Remove inapplicable items, add environment-specific items, and adjust priorities based on your needs.
How do I track results over time?
Document completion dates and any issues found in your ticketing system. This helps identify trends and recurring problems.
