Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

Organizations commonly make these mistakes:

  • Over-engineering: Creating overly complex solutions that are difficult to maintain
  • Insufficient monitoring: Deploying systems without comprehensive observability
  • Inadequate security: Treating security as an afterthought rather than designing for it
  • Poor documentation: Failing to document decisions and procedures
  • Lack of testing: Deploying to production without proper testing
  • Ignoring compliance: Not considering regulatory requirements during design
