Exam Tips & Strategy

Key Success Factors

  • Practice Labs: Hands-on Azure portal experience is critical
  • Read Documentation: Familiarize yourself with Microsoft's official docs
  • Take Multiple Practice Tests: Aim for 80%+ before attempting the exam
  • Understand Concepts: Focus on understanding WHY, not just memorization
  • Time Management: Practice pacing to complete all questions
  • Review Weak Areas: Identify gaps and target additional study
