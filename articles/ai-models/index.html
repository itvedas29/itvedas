❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which AI model is best overall?

There's no single "best" model. Claude 3.5 Sonnet, GPT-4o, and Gemini 2.0 Flash each excel in different areas. Choose based on your specific needs: long context (Gemini), reasoning (Claude), speed (all three), or cost (Gemini).

Can I use open-source models in production?

Yes! Llama 3.1, Mistral, and Qwen are production-ready. They're perfect for applications requiring privacy, customization, or cost control. Performance is excellent for most use cases.

What's the cheapest way to use AI models?

Self-host open-source models (Llama, Mistral) for lowest cost. If using APIs, Gemini 2.0 Flash offers best value at $0.075/M input tokens. Batch processing can further reduce costs.

How do I keep up with new models?

This guide is updated monthly. Subscribe to AI research publications like Papers with Code, follow model announcements on official websites, and check our News section for latest developments.
